People & the environment

A background
across disciplines.

My education combines a master's in Geographic Information Systems and Remote Sensing from Ulster University with a bachelor's degree in human geography from Aberystwyth University.

At SALT, I worked with digital cartography, remote sensing and environmental analysis in marine and coastal projects. My contributions include the technical side of SALT Trail 2.0, marine mapping, conservation communication and the exploration of kelp classification methods.

A geodata placement with Sandefjord Municipality and volunteer GIS work connected to recreational water mapping in Ireland gave me further insight into how geographic information supports practical decisions.

Through Sigrøn, I am developing independent projects around Goksjø water quality and darker skies, while exploring drone applications, LiDAR, photogrammetry and machine learning for geospatial analysis.

Earlier work in health and social care, including team leadership in the UK, shaped the way I communicate, collaborate and approach people’s needs.
